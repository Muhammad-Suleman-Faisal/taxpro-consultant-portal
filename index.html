Frequently Asked Questions

Essential answers for corporate leaders, investors, and taxpayers in Pakistan.

For individuals and salaried persons, the statutory deadline is generally September 30th of each tax year. For corporate entities with a special tax year or December closing, the deadline is typically December 31st. FBR may issue official extension circulars periodically.
Individuals can obtain an NTN using their CNIC, registered mobile SIM, and electricity bill through the FBR Iris portal. ATL (Active Taxpayer List) status is immediately unlocked upon submission of the relevant annual tax return and payment of the applicable ATL surcharge challan.
Income Tax is a direct tax levied on your annual net profit or salary under the Federal Income Tax Ordinance 2001. Sales Tax is an indirect consumption tax levied on goods (under FBR at standard 18%) or services (under provincial authorities like SRB/PRA at 13-16%) on monthly transactions.
We thoroughly evaluate the legal basis of the notice (Sections 122, 177, or 214C), prepare formal documentary reconciliations, draft legal rejoinders referencing case law, and physically represent you before assessing officers to prevent unmerited ex-parte orders.
Every SECP-registered company must conduct an Annual General Meeting (AGM), submit Form A / Form B (annual returns), Form 29 (particulars of directors and officers), and maintain an updated register of Ultimate Beneficial Owners (UBO) under Anti-Money Laundering frameworks.
